Support

How can we help?

Focus Hour is a simple, local-only Pomodoro timer. Most questions are answered below — if not, email us directly.

How does the Pomodoro cycle work?
Focus Hour follows the classic Pomodoro Technique: a focus session, then a short break. After a set number of focus sessions (4 by default), you get a longer break to reset, then the cycle continues. You can change the focus length, break length, sessions until a long break, and the long break length in Settings.
Does Skip count toward my stats or streak?
No. Skip moves you to the next phase without recording it — only sessions that finish naturally count toward your stats, streak, and heatmap.
What happens if I leave the app during a focus session?
Nothing bad — Focus Hour just gently lets you know how many times you stepped away once the session ends. It's a nudge, not a penalty, and nothing is tracked or sent anywhere.
Will I still be notified if I background the app?
Yes. Focus Hour schedules a local notification when you start a session, so you'll be alerted when a focus session or break ends even if you've left the app.
Can I change the color theme?
Yes — open Settings and pick from five themes: Ember, Ocean, Forest, Grape, or Mono.
Do I need an account?
No. Focus Hour has no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. Everything lives only on your device.
How do I reset my stats or streak?
There's currently no in-app reset for history. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all local data, including your streak and heatmap.
Is Focus Hour really free?
Yes — free, with every feature included. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy, simply explained

Last updated: August 5, 2026. This policy applies to the Focus Hour iOS application published by Mark Utility Labs.

🔒

Short version: Focus Hour stores your timer settings and session history only on your device. There's no account, no analytics, and nothing is ever sent off your phone.

1. Overview

Focus Hour is a local-first Pomodoro timer. The only data it creates — your timer settings, session history, streak, and heatmap — is generated and stored entirely on your device.

2. How it's stored

All data is stored locally on your iPhone using standard on-device storage. Deleting the app removes this data completely; nothing is backed up to any server we control.

3. What is NOT collected

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party data SDKs
  • No account or sign-in
  • No network requests carrying your data

4. Permissions

Focus Hour requests permission to send local notifications, so it can alert you when a focus session or break ends — even if the app is in the background. It requests no other permissions: no camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

5. Children's privacy

Focus Hour is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
